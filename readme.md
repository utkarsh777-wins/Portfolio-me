### ❖ Featured Project: [deskBuddy-v1](https://github.com/utkarsh777-wins/deskBuddy-v1) ↗
An intelligent, ambient hardware edge node designed to eliminate digital friction through a robust Finite State Machine (FSM) architecture and on-device intelligence.

* **The Hardware Stack:**
    * **Core:** `ESP32 DevKit V1`
    * **Visuals:** `GC9A01 Round LCD` (Dynamic expressions & Clock UI)
    * **Sensing:** `HC-SR04` (Proximity), `PIR Sensor` (Motion detection), and `Light Sensor` (Ambient adaptation)
    * **Intelligence:** `TinyML Wake Word` detection for low-power voice activation.
    * **Audio:** `I2S Digital Microphone` & `PAM8403 Amplifier`.
* **The Logic:** deskBuddy monitors posture, reacts to environmental lighting, and utilizes PIR motion sensing to "wake up" only when you are present.
* **The Intent:** No tab switching. Use the dedicated **TinyML wake word** or a proximity gesture to trigger high-speed technical assistance through an ambient interface.
